# Real packaging reference for the Cases view

Scope: North American (USA) retail packaging, with PAL/JP differences where known.
Confidence tags: **[src]** = supported by a fetched source (see Sources); **[mem]** = general
collector knowledge NOT verified by any fetched source (treat as approx); **approx** = rough.
Dimensions are W x H x D in mm (front-facing: W = across the cover, H = up the spine, D = spine thickness).
Wikipedia lists "H x W x D" for cases; re-ordered here. Box (cardboard) dimensions found online were
only retailer protector-sleeve sizes, so every cardboard-box figure is **approx**.

## Notes per console

- **Default spine convention (western):** title reads top-to-bottom (head tilted right) for DVD/Blu-ray
  keep cases, jewel cases, and Nintendo boxes. This is **[mem]** - no fetched source stated it.
  JP spines may read the same direction (JP vertical) but are often horizontal on JP Blu-ray/DS cases.
- **GameCube:** black full-size case in NA/EU, clear 149 x 107 x 17 case in Japan and for the NA Game Boy
  Player disc [src]. Mini-DVD with memory-card slot [src]. Player's Choice: yellow box on top of case [src].
- **Wii:** 190 x 135 x 14, white, sometimes coloured [src]. PAL cases same shape; PAL ratings band differs.
- **Wii U:** cerulean/light blue, same 190 x 135 x 14 footprint [src].
- **Switch:** clear case 170 x 104 x 10 [src]. Red-style cases also used on some Switch 1 titles in EU/JP [src].
  Switch 2 red cases with red top banner [src]. Game-key cards: same box, "game-key card" label [src].
- **PS1:** jewel case [src], black tray [src]. NA Greatest Hits uses green banner with red vertical logo [src].
  Longboxes 1995-96 are [mem] (the jewel case article says CDs used longboxes, but not PS1 specifically).
- **PS2:** NA black, PAL navy blue from 2001 [src]; Greatest Hits red from PS2 onward [src]; PAL Platinum line
  is silver [src, applied to PS3 listing] / Platinum [mem for PS2].
- **PSP:** UMD case 177 x 104 x 14 [src]; Wikipedia keep-case page lists case 168 x 99 x 14 clear [src].
  PSP Greatest Hits is red [src].
- **DS:** NA/JP black 135 x 122 x 15, EU clear 20 deep [src]. DSi-only white; some Mario red [src].
  3DS cards have a tab; case similar [src]. 3DS case specifics: unverified.
- **N64:** boxes approx 7 x 5 x 1.25-1.5 in [approx]. NA Player's Choice: yellow background behind N64 logo;
  PAL: silver/platinum [src]. Red spine claim is NOT verified - do not rely on it (mario wiki / wiki text did not state a spine colour).
- **SNES:** boxes approx same footprint as N64 but narrower; Player's Choice uses gold SNES wordmark,
  most boxes redesigned red, Disney blue [src]. NA boxes differ from JP (JP box taller/boxier, flaps).
- **NES:** approx 4.9 x 7 x 0.9 in sample [approx]; box protector listing 5.12 x 7.17 x 1.02 in inside [src].
  Silver/grey colouring is [mem].
- **Game Boy / GBC / GBA:** protector listing 128 x 128 x 25 mm for GB [approx]; JP GB has two sizes, later about 4/5 of
  Famicom box [src]. GBA cartridges 35 x 57 x 7.5 mm (not box) [src].
- **Genesis/Mega Drive:** black plastic clamshell [src, low quality]; no reliable dimensions.

## Quick facts for modelling
- Standard keep case (DVD): 135 x 190 x 14-15 mm. Blu-ray: 135 x 171.5 x 13. Jewel: 125 x 142 x 10. UMD: 99 x 168 x 14 (+ outer 104 x 177).
- Switch/Switch 2 are the thinnest at 10 mm. DS-family 15 mm (NA) / 20 mm (EU).
- Cardboard boxes (N64/SNES) approx 32-38 mm deep; NES approx 22 mm; GB approx 25 mm. All approx.

## What the Cases view draws (and why)

The reference above drives `style_of()` / `spine_look()` in `src/hub/cases_draw.cpp` and `case_dims()` in `src/hub/cases_logic.h`.
Real scans (front, and GameTDB full scans with spine and back where they exist) always win; the rules below only apply to what is drawn around or instead of a scan.

| Console | Plastic / card | Spine (drawn) | Edges, top and bottom | Drawn back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GameCube, PS2, PSP | black (PSP: smoke) | black, light cap band, title top to bottom | plastic colour | black panel |
| Wii U | light blue | blue, white cap band | blue | blue panel |
| Switch | clear plastic around a red-banded sleeve | red | clear | white panel with red banner |
| Switch 2 | red-tinted plastic | red, two-tone cap | red | white panel |
| DS, 3DS | black (NTSC-U) | black, light logo band | black | light panel |
| PS1 | clear jewel case, black tray | black card, PlayStation strip | clear rails | black tray panel |
| N64, SNES, NES, GB, GBC, GBA | printed card in the game's colours (N64 strongest, NES / SNES mildest) | the front art's colours | the front art's colours | panel tinted by the front art |

Spines always read top to bottom (US convention). Cardboard boxes are matte (no sheen); plastic cases are glossy.
